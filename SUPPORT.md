# Getting Support for CarbonSnap

Depending on whether you're self-hosting, contributing code, or exploring managed deployment, here's how to get help.

---

## Community channels

* **GitHub Discussions** — the primary place for Q&A, feature ideas, and factor pack requests.
* **GitHub Issues** — bug reports and verified system errors.
* **Contributors** — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for development help and the [Contributor Guide](docs/technical/contributor-guide.md).

---

## FAQ

### 1. Can I use CarbonSnap free of charge in my business?

Yes. CarbonSnap is released under the **AGPLv3 open-source license** — self-host it on your own server for internal use at zero cost.

### 2. What emission factors are included?

Out of the box: UK DESNZ/DEFRA 2024, US EPA 2024, and IPCC AR6 global factors — see [emission-factors/](emission-factors/).

### 3. Does it support Google sign-in?

Yes — sign in with email/password or Google. First-time Google users get a workspace provisioned automatically.

### 4. Where does my data live?

You choose. Self-hosted instances store data on your own infrastructure; tenant metadata lives in your Firebase project. See [self-hosting.md](docs/guides/self-hosting.md).

### 5. How do I get enterprise or advisory-firm support?

For managed cloud hosting, SLAs, custom factor packs, or white-label advisory portals, contact us through the landing page contact form or open a discussion on GitHub.
